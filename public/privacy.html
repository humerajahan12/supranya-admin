Privacy Policy

App: Supranya (Android) · Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Supranya app collects, why, who sees it, and what choices you have. The app is operated by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("Supranya Green Power Services", "we", "us"), [REGISTERED ADDRESS]. It is used by customers who book EV-charger services and by our own field technicians.

2. Who can see it

  • Our dispatch/admin team can see customer and job details and technician locations and attendance.
  • A customer can see the name and live position of the technician assigned to their own job while it is in progress.
  • Technicians see the name, location and service details of the customers whose jobs they are assigned.

3. Service providers we use

  • Render – hosts our server.
  • Supabase – hosts our database.
  • Razorpay – processes payments.
  • Google – map display (Google Maps) and, on your phone, Android's built-in address lookup.
  • OpenStreetMap-based services – we send route start/end coordinates (without your name or phone number) to a routing service to draw the road route, and address-search text to a geocoding service when your phone cannot resolve it.

Servers are located in [REGION – check your Render and Supabase project regions]. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

4. How long we keep it

  • Customer account data: until you delete your account (see section 5).
  • When you delete your account, your profile, chargers and saved addresses are deleted, and your name and phone number are removed from past service records. A record that a service took place (ticket number, service, charger nickname, location, dates) remains for our business and warranty records. Routine database backups may keep deleted data for up to [30] days.
  • Technician data: for the period of employment/engagement and as required by law afterwards.
  • Server logs: up to [30] days.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app go to Profile → Delete my account. You can also request deletion without the app – see /delete-account.html.
  • Correct or get a copy of your information: contact us at the address below.
  • Location: you can turn off location permission in your phone's settings. Customers can still use the app by searching for or dragging the pin to an address. Technicians need location on for live job tracking.
  • You may also have rights under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Contact our grievance officer below to exercise them.

6. Security

PINs are stored only as hashes; accounts lock after repeated wrong PINs; sessions can be ended by our support team; access to the admin dashboard requires a login. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
