Delete your Supranya account

Supranya (by SUPRANYA GREEN POWER SERVICES) lets you delete your account and data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Supranya app and sign in.
  2. Go to Home → Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account, enter your PIN and confirm.

If you still have an open service request, finish it first or contact support; we cannot delete an account with work in progress.

Option 2: without the app

Contact us from the mobile number registered on the account:

Email: support@supranyagreenpowerservices.com (subject "Delete my account") · Phone: +91 99499 57744

We will call the registered number to confirm it is you, then delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your profile (name, phone number, PIN), registered chargers and saved addresses.
  • Anonymised: your name and phone number are removed from past service records.
  • Kept: a record that a service took place (ticket number, service type, charger nickname, location and dates) for our business and warranty records.
  • Routine backups may hold deleted data for up to 30 days before they expire.
